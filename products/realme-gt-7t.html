Product Overview

The realme GT 7T is a performance-focused smartphone designed for users who want flagship-level speed, an immersive display, and exceptional battery life. Powered by the MediaTek Dimensity 8400-Max processor, it delivers smooth multitasking, high-end gaming, and efficient everyday performance. The brilliant 1.5K AMOLED display produces vibrant visuals, while the massive 7000mAh battery ensures extended usage without frequent charging. Combined with ultra-fast 120W SUPERVOOC charging and a premium design, the realme GT 7T offers an outstanding flagship experience at a competitive price.
