How We Test & Score Devices

Every device rating in our catalog is derived from standard benchmark parameters:

Compute & GPU

Sustained thermal throttling loops, Antutu 10 scores, and Geekbench single/multi core metrics.

Optics & Zoom

Sensor size, aperture, OIS stability, low-light noise binning, and 4K/8K video bitrate quality.

Endurance Curve

Standard 150-nit web browsing, 60fps video playback, and 0-100% charging cycle times.

Value Ratio

Hardware spec delivery calculated directly against launch MSRP across all 7 budget tiers.
