BUYING TIPS

Choosing the right smartphone depends on your budget and daily needs. Keep these 5 tips in mind.

1. BUDGET

Decide your budget first before comparing phones.

2. PROCESSOR

Choose a processor based on daily apps or gaming.

3. CAMERA

Look at sensor size and OIS, not just megapixels.

4. BATTERY

Check both capacity (mAh) and charging speed.

5. BRAND

Pick brands with good local service and updates.
