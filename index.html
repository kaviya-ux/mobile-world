Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our specifications portal

How often are smartphone specifications updated?
Our database is updated in real-time as global smartphone launch events happen and official white papers are published. We ensure benchmark ratings, sensor sizes, and price tiers reflect the current market.
What does the "Mobile World Rating" mean?
It is an aggregate, weighted evaluation combining CPU/GPU synthetic benchmarks, camera low-light sensor metrics, battery endurance hours, and display color accuracy against the device's retail price.
Can I compare two phones side-by-side?
Yes! You can explore individual product spec pages and click the "Compare" button on any phone card, or jump to our Best Specs leaderboards to see top performers compared side-by-side.
Do you sell mobile phones directly?
No, Mobile World is an independent informational and research portal. We do not sell hardware directly to ensure 100% unbiased reviews and recommendations.
