Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our specs portal

How often are Mobile specs updated?
Our database is updated daily as new devices are announced and benchmarked. We source data directly from manufacturer white papers and verified testing labs.
What does the "Mobile World Rating" mean?
It's an aggregate score based on processor performance, display quality, camera output, and battery life, weighted against the device's launch price to determine value.
Can I compare two Mobiles side-by-side?
Yes! Simply use the 'View Details' button on any phone card and look for the 'Compare' option on the device specification page.
Do you sell the Mobile directly?
No, Mobile World is an information portal. We provide links to trusted retailers, but we do not sell hardware directly to ensure our reviews remain unbiased.
